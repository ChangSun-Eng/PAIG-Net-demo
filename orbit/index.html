Surface rendering and native times

The two plates share the same undeformed surface mesh, camera and field scale. The colours use bilinear interpolation within the original surface quadrilaterals. The 120 records use 4,096 display levels across each field’s fixed range. Temperature rise is relative to 400 °C. Equivalent strain is the instantaneous deviatoric equivalent of total logarithmic strain. Von Mises stress uses the elastic-strain constitutive route in PAIG-Net. The thermal and mechanical fields retain their respective heat-transfer and stress-analysis clocks.
